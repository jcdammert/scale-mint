# Scale Mint Repositioning Brief

## The shift
Scale Mint is repositioning from "software platform" to "marketing agency."
Smintos (the field service software) is being deprioritized and is not to be
referenced anywhere on the live site for now.

## Core differentiator (use this to guide all copy)
Scale Mint is not just an ads agency and not just a software vendor. The pitch
is: we run the marketing (ads, SEO, website) AND we own the sales process that
turns those leads into booked jobs — missed call text back, automated
follow up, and either a trained AI setter, a plugged in sales rep, or training
for the client's own team. Marketing without a sales process just generates
leads that go cold; that combination is the actual offer. Website and SEO
remain part of the marketing side, not a separate thing.

## What this means in copy and structure
- Never use the words "platform," "install," or "the system" as the primary
  subject of a sentence. Lead with the outcome (more leads, more booked jobs,
  higher rankings), not the software.
- Nav item "Platform" becomes "Services."
- "Log in" moves out of the primary nav (footer or a small secondary link is
  fine) — it should not compete with the main booking CTA.
- Any comparison to field-service SOFTWARE competitors (Jobber, Housecall Pro,
  etc.) gets cut or reframed as "agency vs. software" positioning, not
  "platform vs. platform."
- The follow up/pipeline pillar stays prominent — it's not internal
  infrastructure, it's the "sales process" half of the core differentiator.
- Ads, SEO, and website content should read as core marketing services, not
  as a bolt-on add-on mentioned last.
- Cut the "Backed by NVIDIA Inception / Microsoft for Startups / Google for
  Startups" badges from the homepage entirely for now — startup/SaaS
  signaling works against this positioning.

## Tone
Direct, clean, natural. No hyphens in public-facing copy. Avoid
AI-sounding, overly wordy language.

This brief is the reference for every page change going forward. Point back
to it instead of re-deriving positioning on each prompt.
